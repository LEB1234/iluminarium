What is a zine?

Zines are traditionally 2d printed photocopies from a master copy, which could have been made using collage, screen printing, or any variety of medium suited for the work. What makes zines unique is that these works are self-published by individuals who have something important to share.

There are tons of ways to share your ideas and creativity. If you're just starting out in making art or putting your awesome thoughts out there, a good place to begin is with stuff you're already familiar with. Check out the list of different approaches below, see what you know, what's new to you, and what you've never heard of. You'll find a variety of mediums and techniques for making zines, along with examples of artists' work.
